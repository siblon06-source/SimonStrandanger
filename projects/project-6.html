Projekt 06 · 3D-modellering

Polygon modelling in Blender

Two hard-surface objects, modelled polygon by polygon: a replica of DJI's Osmo Pocket 3 and a wristwatch made for Ozia Watches. Both come down to the same craft: clean topology that holds its shape under subdivision, and materials that make the surfaces read as metal and glass.

Verktyg
Blender
Metod
Subdivision
Objekt
Kamera, klocka

01 Osmo Pocket 3

A replica of the DJI handheld camera. The wireframes show how the edge flow follows the form around the lens, the gimbal arm and the housing, so the highlights stay smooth.

The camera replica was built in clay-style views first, to judge shape and proportions before any material was added.

02 Ozia Watches

The same approach applied to a wristwatch, made for Ozia Watches as material for marketing and branding. The model required quite complex topology and had to be redone a few times.

The node-based system that controls the material on the watch wristband's surface.
